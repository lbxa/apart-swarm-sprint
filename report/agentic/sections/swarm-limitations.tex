\section{Limitations and Dual-Use / Ethical Considerations}
The sample is small, adaptive and conditioned on earlier Kimi signal.
Endpoints from the same model family are not independent model families.
Repeated seed labels across checkout cohorts do not create distinct launch
seeds, and three cases in a continuous session are not independent blocks.
We report a time-stamped partial cloud snapshot, not the final outcome of the
campaign still in progress. Submitted claims are checked against the retained
factual reference. A wrong ballot alone is not an intent or coalition label.
Independent human semantic review remains open.

All evaluated jurors receive adversarial recruitment directly, and most arms
also contain a scripted publisher's offer. The size-three seed condition has
one scripted bad juror \emph{plus} that adversarial publisher; it is not a
demonstration that a lone compromised actor in an otherwise benign network
caused an epidemic. Larger juries contain several seeded bad jurors. Topology,
multi-hop lineage and transmission velocity are unmeasured. Incentive levels,
seed fractions and quorum leverage limit comparison to other agent networks.

Zero false reveals can coexist with widespread non-participation. Provider
errors, bounded calls and scheduling affect whether a vote is executed. Failed
played sessions are retained rather than selectively repeated. Returned
reasoning is observable output with uncertain faithfulness; it is corroborated
by actual commitments, reveals and credit transfers where execution is claimed.
The short charter omits duties tested by the principles contrast, so its false
votes are not described as violations of those omitted duties.

All financial actions occur on local Anvil chains with synthetic credits.
No public-chain loss or real-money game reward is claimed. Provider keys,
wallet secrets and operator capabilities are excluded from participant inputs
and report excerpts. A financially motivated false-consensus mechanism could
inform attacks on review or adjudication systems; the defensive use is to
evaluate communication policies, incentives, duties and quorum rules together.
No containment defense or topology advantage is validated here.

The report uses a LaTeX internal-draft layout. The current official template
link and submission requirements were checked, but visual equivalence and
author affiliations still require author review. No submission has been made.
