# Deliver inspection obligations before evaluating juror decisions

- **Date:** 2026-11-01
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Deciders:** Benchmark owner, through research-design authority delegated in this session
- **Area:** harness, benchmark, research

## Context

The voucher inspection standard was mounted as `standard.md` but absent from
the opening reading instructions. Availability alone does not establish that a
participant saw the obligations before acting. The benchmark distinguishes
knowing coordination from missed instructions and verification errors.

## Decision

Include the complete existing inspection standard in the initial system prompt
whenever a lookup allowance is configured, and link its mounted copy from the
records-service guide. Repeat both verification conditions under this version.
Retain the complete earlier verification-alone cohort as an instruction-discovery
version, with its observations and costs intact.

Use an explicit primary assignment registry: the four unaffected factual v3
conditions and both revised verification v4 conditions. Record actual prompt
exposure, individual ballots and inspection before commitment in the offline
audit. Missing paid-request records must fail reproduction rather than silently
reduce costs.

## Consequences

The revised voucher test measures behavior with an explicitly delivered rule.
It does not measure discovery of that rule. Factual prompts remain unchanged;
their hashes are checked before and after the change. One session per cell and
two dependent cases remain exploratory evidence, without a model ranking.

Two supplemental conditions use the existing client-plan pressure and a feasible
10 ETH local-credit offer. The first retains the principles constitution; the
second uses the latest short root constitution as a mechanism calibration. The
root charter omits the tested truth and independent-judgment duties, so its
financial coordination cannot establish violation of those omitted duties.
Both supplemental conditions remain separate from the primary registry.

See the [campaign protocol](../../harness/research/frontier-2026-10-31/PROTOCOL.md)
and [offline analysis](../../harness/research/frontier-2026-10-31/analyze.py).
