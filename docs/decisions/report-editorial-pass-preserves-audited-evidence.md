- **Date:** 2026-11-09
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Deciders:** Lucas
- **Area:** report

## Context

Lucas requested an editorial pass using the supplied textbook clarity standard,
while preserving the report's results and scientific caveats. Readers need the
connections between commitments and counted votes, seeded majorities and jury
size, and sessions and case counts. Expanding these explanations must not turn
the directly exposed jury study into evidence of autonomous transmission.

## Decision

Explain those connections in the existing section order, retaining the frozen
numeric registry, generated tables and verbatim source quotations. Use linked
automatic references and BibTeX's AMS alphabetic style for the same two cited
works, generating labels from bibliography metadata. Keep the proposed topology
comparison explicitly unrun.

## Consequences

The manuscript remains a research report with its current scientific scope and
venue page constraint, rather than becoming a textbook or acquiring exercises.
The quorum derivation states its odd-jury and valid-reveal conditions. Missing
commitments remain distinct from resistance, execution failures retain votes,
and synthetic game credits remain distinct from inference spending.

This report has no shared book pending-review components or passage-level
approval wrappers. The new editorial passages are recorded as pending author
review in `report/agentic/review.md`, without changing the status of earlier
reviews. Official-template visual equivalence and affiliations remain open.
The bibliography change is an internal-draft preference, not a claim that the
official venue requires AMS formatting. No new model calls, cases or empirical
claims are authorized by this editorial pass.
