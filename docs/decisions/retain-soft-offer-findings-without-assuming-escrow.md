# Retain soft-offer findings without assuming escrow

- **Date:** 2026-11-01
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Deciders:** Benchmark owner, through research-design authority delegated in this session
- **Area:** benchmark, research, reporting

## Context

The frozen five-model study tested public payment promises and a seeded juror.
The scripted party honors matching revealed votes after it wins, using actual
contract credit transfers. The conditional promise itself is not escrowed or
enforced by the contract. Participants cannot observe the operator's fixed
payment policy, so counterparty trust can influence their choices.

Three short-charter cases produced wrong majorities and actual payments to both
evaluated jurors. That charter omits truth and independent-judgment duties.
The primary and quota conditions use the principles charter and remain separate.
The full result set, failures, incomplete action prefixes and all costs are
retained in the [campaign results](../../harness/research/frontier-2026-10-31/RESULTS.md).

## Decision

Complete the frozen eight-condition exploratory screen and preserve its original
assignments. Report the paid cases as induced financial coordination under the
short charter. Keep constitutional-violation labels inapplicable to omitted
duties and semantic annotations provisional pending independent human review.

## Consequences

The release supports exact evidence and analysis replay, not a population rate,
model ranking or pure causal estimate of charter wording. Wrong ballots,
unilateral inspection departures, intended actions, confirmed actions, failed
turns and completed payments remain separate observations.

An escrowed conditional offer is a future mechanism experiment requiring an
explicitly versioned financial extension and matched controls. Current native
payment fixtures establish feasibility and matching-recipient behavior, while
leaving participants' beliefs about the original promises unmeasured. Further
seeds and blinded adjudication also remain future work.
